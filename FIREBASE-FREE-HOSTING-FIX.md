# 🔧 Firebase Billing Issue - FREE Solutions

## 🚨 **The Problem:**
Firebase is asking for billing because you're trying to use "App Hosting" instead of regular "Hosting".

## ✅ **Solution 1: Use Firebase Hosting (FREE)**

### **Find the RIGHT Hosting Option:**
1. **Go to**: https://console.firebase.google.com/project/heritagelink-22d0f
2. **Look in left sidebar** for:
   - ✅ **"Hosting"** (FREE - use this one)
   - ❌ **"App Hosting"** (Paid - avoid this)

### **Steps:**
1. **Click**: "Hosting" (not App Hosting)
2. **Click**: "Get started"
3. **Skip**: CLI setup (click "Continue to console")
4. **Click**: "Add another site" or "Deploy"
5. **Upload**: Your `public/index.html` file
