# Artisan Messaging Guide

## How to Reply to Customer Messages

### Step 1: Access Messages
1. Login to your artisan account
2. Click **"Messages"** in the sidebar
3. You'll see your messages page with two panels

### Step 2: View Conversations
**Left Panel - Conversation List**
- Shows all customers who have messaged you
- Shows all customers you've messaged
- Displays the last message preview
- Shows when the conversation started

**What You'll See:**
- 👤 Customer name
- 💬 Last message preview (first 50 characters)
- 📅 Date of last message

### Step 3: Select a Conversation
1. Click on any customer name in the left panel
2. The right panel will load the full conversation
3. You'll see all messages exchanged with that customer

### Step 4: Read Messages
**Right Panel - Message View**
- **Left side (light green)**: Messages from the customer
- **Right side (gradient green)**: Your replies
- Messages are shown in chronological order (oldest first)
- Each message shows the date and time

### Step 5: Send a Reply
1. Type your message in the text box at the bottom
2. Click the **"Send"** button (or press Enter)
3. Your message will appear immediately in the conversation
4. A green notification will confirm "Message sent successfully!"

### Step 6: Continue Conversation
- You can send multiple messages
- The customer will receive your replies
- New messages from the customer will appear in the conversation
- Refresh the page to see new incoming messages

## Tips for Effective Communication

### Professional Responses
✅ **Do:**
- Greet customers warmly
- Answer questions clearly
- Provide detailed product information
- Respond within 24 hours
- Be polite and professional
- Thank customers for their interest

❌ **Don't:**
- Use informal or slang language
- Ignore messages
- Give incomplete information
- Be rude or dismissive
- Share personal contact information publicly

### Sample Responses

#### Product Inquiry
```

This basket is made from locally-sourced bamboo using traditional 
weaving techniques passed down through generations. It measures 
30cm x 30cm x 20cm and is perfect for storage or decoration.

The price is ₱750, and I can customize the size or color upon request. 
Would you like to place an order?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

```

Based on your requirements, I can make a [product description] 
in [specifications]. The estimated completion time is [timeframe], 
and the price would be ₱[amount].

I'll need a 50% deposit to begin work. Would you like to proceed?

Please let me know if you have any questions!
```

#### Order Confirmation
```
Thank you for your order!

I've received your payment and will begin working on your 
[product name] right away. I'll send you progress photos 
and notify you when it's ready for pickup/delivery.

Expected completion: [date]

Looking forward to creating this piece for you!
```

#### Delivery Information
```
Your order is ready!

You can pick it up at [location] during [hours], or I can 
arrange delivery to [area] for an additional ₱[amount].

Please let me know which option works best for you.
```

## Message Management

### Organizing Conversations
- Conversations are automatically sorted by most recent
- Active conversation is highlighted in green
- Click any conversation to switch between customers
- All message history is preserved

### Checking for New Messages
1. Go to Messages page
2. Look for conversations at the top of the list
3. New messages will appear in the conversation view
4. Refresh the page to see latest messages

### Following Up
- If a customer hasn't responded in a few days, send a friendly follow-up
- Ask if they need any additional information
- Remind them of your availability
- Keep the conversation professional

## Troubleshooting

### Message Won't Send
**Problem**: Click Send but nothing happens

**Solutions:**
1. Check that you've typed a message
2. Make sure you're logged in
3. Refresh the page and try again
4. Check your internet connection

### Can't See Conversation
**Problem**: Conversation list is empty

**Solutions:**
1. Wait for customers to message you first
2. Check if you have any messages in your email
3. Make sure your profile is complete and visible
4. Promote your products to attract customers

### Message Appears Twice
**Problem**: Same message shows up multiple times

**Solutions:**
1. Don't click Send multiple times
2. Wait for the success notification
3. Refresh the page to see the correct view

### Customer Not Responding
**Problem**: Sent a reply but customer hasn't answered

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 24-48 hours for a response
2. Send a polite follow-up message
3. Check if your message was clear and complete
4. Some customers may be browsing and will respond later

## Privacy & Security

### What to Share
✅ **Safe to share:**
- Product information
- Prices and payment methods
- Pickup/delivery locations (general area)
- Business hours
- Product availability

❌ **Never share:**
- Personal phone number (use platform messaging)
- Home address (use pickup points)
- Bank account details (use secure payment methods)
- Personal social media accounts
- Other customers' information

### Reporting Issues
If a customer is:
- Harassing or threatening
- Requesting inappropriate content
- Attempting to scam
- Being abusive

**Action**: Contact the HeritageLink administrator immediately

## FAQ

**Q: Can I delete messages?**
A: Currently, messages are permanent for record-keeping. Contact admin if you need a message removed.

**Q: Can customers see my other conversations?**
A: No, each conversation is private between you and that specific customer.

**Q: How do I know if a customer read my message?**
A: Read receipts are coming in a future update. For now, wait for their reply.

**Q: Can I send images in messages?**
A: Image attachments are coming soon. For now, add images to your product listings or portfolio.

**Q: What if I'm away and can't respond?**
A: Set up an auto-reply message (coming soon) or update your profile with your availability.

**Q: Can I message customers first?**
A: Currently, customers initiate conversations. You can reply to any customer who has messaged you.
