# Artisan Product Management Guide

## Adding a New Product

1. **Login** to your artisan account
2. Navigate to **"My Products"** from the sidebar
3. Click the **"Add Product"** button (yellow button in top right)
4. Fill in the product form:

### Required Fields
- **Product Name**: The name of your craft (e.g., "Handwoven Basket")
- **Description**: Detailed description including materials, cultural significance, and craftsmanship
- **Price**: Price in Philippine Pesos (₱)

### Optional Fields
- **Category**: Type of craft (e.g., Textiles, Pottery, Wood Carving, Bamboo Crafts)
- **Product Images**: Upload up to 5 images
  - Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP
  - Maximum file size: 5MB per image
  - First image will be used as the main product image

5. Click **"Add Product"** to save

## Uploading Product Images

### Method 1: Click to Upload
1. Click on the upload area (with cloud icon)
2. Select images from your computer
3. Preview will show selected images
4. Submit the form to save

### Method 2: Drag and Drop
1. Drag image files from your computer
2. Drop them onto the upload area
3. Preview will show selected images
4. Submit the form to save

### Image Tips
- Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- Show different angles of your product
- Include close-ups of details and craftsmanship
- Use a clean, uncluttered background
- First image is most important (main display)

## Editing a Product

1. Go to **"My Products"**
2. Find the product you want to edit
3. Click the **"Edit"** button (green button)
4. Update any fields you want to change
5. Upload new images to replace existing ones (optional)
6. Click **"Update Product"** to save changes

## Deleting a Product

1. Go to **"My Products"**
2. Find the product you want to delete
3. Click the **"Delete"** button (red button)
4. Confirm the deletion
5. Product will be permanently removed

## Product Status

Products have two status options:
- **Available**: Product is in stock and can be ordered
- **Sold Out**: Product is currently out of stock

## Viewing Your Products

Your products are displayed in a grid layout showing:
- Product image (or placeholder if no image)
- Product name
- Description (first 100 characters)
- Price in ₱
- Status badge
- Edit and Delete buttons

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Add Product
- Make sure all required fields are filled
- Check that you're logged in as an artisan
- Verify your internet connection

### Image Won't Upload
- Check file size (must be under 5MB)
- Verify file format (JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP only)
- Try a different image
- Check your internet connection

### Product Not Showing
- Refresh the page
- Check if product was saved successfully (look for success message)
- Verify you're viewing "My Products" page
